kind: fix

Conflict handling keeps owner approvals and reconciles out-of-band fixes.
`review <pr> owner` and `approve` refuse before any write while the PR conflicts
with its base or an active conflict resolution record exists, so a handover no
longer interrupts a dispatched fixer. Dispatches and escalations never move a
card out of Approved: they report a found approval, the escalation keeps the
column, the resolution carries it on merge, and the post-resolution restore
keeps an owner approval found while the fixer ran instead of returning the
stored pre-conflict turn. Reconcile closes records whose PR is merged or
closed—or whose PR stands mergeable without a fix the record dispatched—quietly
as `conflict on #n resolved outside the fixer` instead of failing until the
record is removed by hand.
