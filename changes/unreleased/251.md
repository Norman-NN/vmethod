kind: fix

Conflict handling keeps owner approvals and reconciles out-of-band fixes.
`review <pr> owner` and `approve` refuse before any write while the PR conflicts
with its base or an active conflict resolution record exists, so a handover no
longer interrupts a dispatched fixer. Dispatches and escalations never move a
card out of Approved: they report a found approval, the escalation keeps the
column, and the resolution carries it on merge. Reconcile closes records whose
PR is merged or closed—or whose PR stands mergeable without a fix the record
dispatched—quietly as `conflict on #n resolved outside the fixer` instead of
failing until the record is removed by hand.
