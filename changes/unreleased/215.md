kind: feature

`learnings aggregate` makes one model call per view so cheap models can aggregate: a family call receives only that family's records and annotations and cannot cite another family's delivery, and the by-domain call sees every delivery. A view fails alone with `<view>: <error>`; the other views are still validated and written, and the run ends with `N views generated, M failed (<views>)` and a non-zero exit when M > 0.
