kind: feature

Scope the orchestrator role per repository: a pinned orchestrator is authoritative only in the repositories its `repos` binding lists and acts as a collaborator everywhere else. Peer project orchestrators can still open issues, discuss and submit learnings, but their comments now void the trivial-issue scope shortcut, their `[orchestrator]` tags are never authoritative for workers, they never count as the orchestrator turn for assignment or board-violation checks, and watch events label them `(collaborator)`. The NOT PLANNED requester event still recognizes them through the binding.
